# Rank Tracker MCP Server: prompts to copy

Paste these into Claude, Claude Code or Cursor once the server is set up with `SEMUST_MCP_TOOLSETS=rank,aiRank`. If you have several Semust projects, name the one you mean, or the assistant will ask. Dates are worked out for you; say "last 30 days" or "last month".

## Morning check

- Give me my ranking overview: visibility score, average position, yesterday's changes.
- Which keywords moved the most since yesterday, up and down?

## Winners and losers

- List the 15 keywords that lost the most positions in 30 days.
- Which keywords climbed the most in the last 7 days on desktop?
- Show keywords that fell out of the top three.

## Distribution and opportunities

- How many keywords are in the top three, and how many in 11–20?
- Which keywords sit in positions 11–20? Those are my page-one candidates.
- List keywords that don't rank at all. Where should I start?

## Trends

- How has my average position changed over 90 days? Any clear break?
- Did my average position change after the last site update? Compare mobile and desktop.
- Show desktop rankings for keywords containing "price".

## AI Overviews

- For how many tracked queries am I cited in an AI Overview?
- Which domains get cited in AI Overviews more often than mine?
- Which tracked queries cite me in AI Overviews, and at which position?
- What is my AI Overview visibility score, and which queries carry it?
